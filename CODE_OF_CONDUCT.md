# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Protect private information and security reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, publishing private information, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This concise policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and may be replaced with the full Contributor Covenant before publication if the maintainers prefer.
